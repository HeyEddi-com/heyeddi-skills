# Examples

## Dashboard

Prompt: "Build the billing dashboard."

1. `@quasar-foundation` for `<q-layout>`, `<q-header>`, and `<q-drawer>`.
2. `@primevue-aura` for `<DataTable>`, filters, and `<Tag>` status.
3. `@aura-design-tokens` for surfaces and text.
4. `@motion-physics` `staggerItem` on the metric row and `modalEnter` on the invoice dialog.
5. `@visual-verification` screenshots `http://localhost:5173/#/billing` at 1440×900 and 390×844, then fixes what the image shows.
