# Vocabulary

- **microFeedback:** hover scale 1.02 and press scale 0.97. Springs, not durations.
- **modalEnter:** dialog and panel entry. Stiffness 280, damping 22.
- **staggerItem:** list or panel entry. 35ms per index, total delay capped at 200ms.
- **Spatial property:** `x`, `y`, or `scale`. These always use a spring.
