# Anti-patterns

- NEVER put an invented number, quote, screen, or feature on screen as if it were real.
- NEVER run `publish`, `cloud`, `auth`, or `events`, or call a paid generator, on your own.
- NEVER say a video is done from a passing check alone: open the frames of the finished file.
- NEVER claim the sound is right. It was measured, not heard.
- NEVER ship AI prose slop on screen or in the report; write plainly: no em dashes, no filler, no hype words.

- Adding a pack to the inventory on a guess about its licence. Some sources mix CC0 with licences that forbid reselling: read the pack's own page.
- Passing a direction to a cloned voice, or choosing a mood the character has no clip for.
